# Catering Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

## 1. Order Confirmation
- All catering orders must be confirmed with the client at least **48 hours** before the event.
- Confirmation must include final head count and dietary restrictions.

## 2. Ingredient Sourcing
- Fresh ingredients (meat, vegetables) must be purchased no more than **24 hours** before food preparation.
- Quality check must be logged upon delivery from suppliers.

## 3. Food Preparation & Safety
- Cold storage items must be kept at or below **4°C**.
- Hot food must be maintained at a minimum of **60°C** during transport.

## 4. Delivery & Setup
- Team must arrive at the venue at least **90 minutes** before the scheduled service time.
- Final setup must be completed **15 minutes** before guests arrive.

## 5. Staff Conduct
- All staff must wear clean uniforms and hairnets.
- No personal mobile phones allowed in the preparation area.
